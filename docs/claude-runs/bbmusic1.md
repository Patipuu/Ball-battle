# BB-MUSIC1: new music set in one key, no more overlap between music and jingles

Status: **implemented, uncommitted.** View and Editor only; no Sim, Rules or save change.

## What Phu heard ("nhạc menu nhạc result hơi bị chồng lấn và hơi kì")
- **`music_result` was 1.26 s long and played as a loop.** On every result and Summary screen it repeated one short C-major chord, so it sounded like a stutter.
- **Keys did not match:**
  - menu ≈ A minor, battle ≈ A minor, boss ≈ E/C;
  - result = a C-major chord;
  - `fight_win` ≈ G/F, `fight_lose` ≈ G/Bb (C minor), `run_over` ≈ Eb/C (C minor).
  - Whenever two of them met, they clashed.
- **Timing:** the result screen started `music_result` at once with a 0.6 s crossfade from the fight track, while the 0.9–2.2 s win/lose jingle played 0.5 s later on top of it. The run-end jingle and the unlock chimes also played over the music at full level.

## What changed
- **New music, `Editor/MusicForge.cs`** (synthesised, deterministic, regenerated by **BallBattle → Forge Sfx and Music**). Everything is in **A minor / C major** (same notes), so any track, any next track and any jingle fit together:

| clip | tempo / length | character |
|---|---|---|
| `music_menu` | 100 bpm, 16 bars, 38.4 s | calm: arpeggio, soft pad, singing lead after a 4-bar intro, light beat (Am F C G / F G Em Am …) |
| `music_battle` | 132 bpm, 16 bars, 29.1 s | driving: eighth-note bass, four-on-the-floor, short hook, fill into the loop |
| `music_boss` | 144 bpm, 16 bars, 26.7 s | darker: A minor with Bb (phrygian), heavier drums, E-major turn into the loop |
| `music_result` | 88 bpm, 8 bars, 21.8 s | a quiet bed: pad, slow chimes, no drums and no hook, so it never competes with a jingle |

- **Loops are seamless:** each loop is rendered twice and the second pass is kept, so note and reverb tails cross the loop point. The jump at the loop point measures the same as at any other bar start.
- **New jingles:** `fight_start`, `fight_win`, `fight_lose`, `run_win`, `run_over`, `unlock`, `retry_banner`.
  - All are 1.6–2.4 s and end on A minor or C major.
  - The win jingles rise (C E G C), the lose jingles fall to A minor.
- **When music plays** (`AudioDirector`, `MusicMixer`):
  - **`music_result` waits:** it is chosen at once, but stays silent until the pending win/lose jingle has finished (+0.2 s), then fades in over 1.5 s. This also works when the result screen opens one frame before the match-end event (`MusicMixer.HoldIncoming`).
  - **A draw** (no jingle) fades `music_result` in at once.
  - **Any jingle** (fight start/win/lose, run win/over, unlock, retry) dips the music by 9 dB while it plays, and the music comes back over 0.4 s.
  - The fight track still fades out when the result jingle plays (as before). Menu ↔ fight crossfades are unchanged (0.6 s, equal power), and now stay in key.
- **Loudness:** music is a little quieter than before (RMS −14 to −19 dB, was −10 to −13 dB), so the hit sounds and jingles stay clear on top.
- **Preview** (`bbmusic1/transitions-preview.wav`, 36 s): menu → fight with `fight_start` → match ends → `fight_win` → `music_result` fades in. The game's own new clips are in `Assets/Audio/music_*.wav`.

## Tests
- **`BbMusic1Tests`, 6 tests:**
  - every music clip is a real loop (> 15 s) and every jingle is < 2.6 s;
  - the result track is silent under the win jingle, then fades in fully;
  - the same holds when the screen changes before the match-end event (lose);
  - a draw fades in at once;
  - any jingle dips the music and a click does not;
  - `HoldIncoming` only works before a track is heard.
- **Older audio tests** (Bb03, BbFix1b, BbSfx2, BbView3) keep their expectations: `music_result` is still the current track at once and starts once, the equal-power crossfade is unchanged, and the fight music still fades out on the result cue.
