# BB-SFX4: cleaner, warmer match sound + new feedback cues

Status: **implemented, uncommitted.** View and Editor only; no Sim, Rules or save change.

## Why it sounded cluttered ("rối")
- **The per-weapon hit clips were hiss.** Share of energy above 6 kHz:

| hit clip | old length | old energy above 6 kHz |
|---|---|---|
| FANG | 0.12 s | 87% |
| ANGLER | 0.32 s | 94% |
| SPARKLER | 0.30 s | 99% |
| MIRROR | 0.50 s | 66% |
| HIVE | 0.30 s | 32% |
| SPORE | 0.35 s | 31% |

- **Hits are the most frequent sound.** A FANG combo, a SPARKLER volley (5–9 arrows) or HIVE turrets started many of these long, bright clips within 100 ms. Wall bounces, turret fire and ball bounces played on top. Up to 12 voices stacked into one hiss.

## What changed
- **New sound set, `Editor/SfxForge.cs`** (deterministic synth, regenerated by **BallBattle → Forge Sfx (SFX4)**):
  - Every hit is now **short** (65–240 ms) and **warm**: under 1% of its energy above 6 kHz, yet a real 1–5 kHz part so it carries on phone speakers.
  - Each ball keeps one colour:
    - EMBER: steel ring on a thud;
    - FANG: tiny dry tick;
    - ANGLER: woody thwack with a plucked line;
    - WRECKER: saturated boom;
    - SPARKLER: light arrow tup;
    - SPORE: wet squelch;
    - MIRROR: short gong;
    - HIVE: small pop.
  - **spike_hit:** metal clank.
  - **parry:** a clear two-note bell (C6 + G6) instead of a metallic hiss.
  - **knockout:** a deep boom with a short falling tune.
  - **generic hit:** warm thud-click.
  - The tonal cues use one key (C major pentatonic), so they never clash.
  - Hits are dry; only the tonal cues get a small room. Echoes on hits read as a flam.
- **New cues:**

| cue | when |
|---|---|
| `tap_strike` | a tap strike (punchy whump) |
| `tap_perfect` | PERFECT! (bright rising chime E6 → A6 over the strike) |
| `tap_miss` | MISS (soft air whiff) |
| `chest_open` | the chest lid opens (wood thunk + sparkle run); also on SKIP |
| `chest_item` | each reward row appears (coin ting) |
| `level_up` | UPGRADE succeeded (chiptune fanfare); a failed UPGRADE plays `ui_denied` |
| `tree_add` | a tree node bought (plink up); a locked or unaffordable node plays `ui_denied` |
| `tree_reset` | RESET with points spent (three falling notes); nothing to reset plays a plain click |

  - `tap_strike`, `tap_perfect`, `chest_open` and `level_up` duck the music like hits do.
- **Thinner mix** (`AudioDirector`):
  - **Hit budget:** at most **3** hit cues start per **120 ms**, of which at most **2** are projectile hits. A 7-arrow volley is now 2 sounds and leaves room for a melee hit.
  - A **plain wall or ball bounce** within 70 ms after a hit cue is not played (the hit covers it), and at most one bounce plays per 60 ms. A **damaging spike** is always heard, and bumper hits are unchanged.
  - **Turret volleys** (ProjectileFired): one soft tick per 250 ms at volume 0.07 (was every volley at 0.12).
  - **Volumes:** projectile hits 0.35 → 0.30; ball bounce 0.35 → 0.22. The tap strike counts as the hit of that moment.
  - The old per-tick rules (same cue once per tick, max 3 hits per tick, spike replaces wall) are unchanged.
- **Demo** (`bbsfx4/`), the same 3-second busy fight (FANG combos, 3 SPARKLER volleys, HIVE turrets, 50 bounces, a parry, a spike, a knockout) mixed both ways:
  - `before-busy-fight.wav`: old clips and rules, **112** cues;
  - `after-busy-fight.wav`: SFX4 clips and rules, **57** cues;
  - `sfx4-sampler.wav`: every new cue in order: blade, fang, pike, brawler, volley, venom, aegis, rig hits, spike, generic hit, parry, knockout, tap strike, PERFECT, MISS, chest open, chest item, level up, tree add, tree reset.
- **Kept:**
  - music, UI clicks, card, fight start/win/lose, run win/over, unlock, skill and boss cues;
  - the generic wall thump (already low and short);
  - the old synth functions stay as `HitClassic` / `ParryClassic` / `DeathClassic` for reference.

## Tests
- **`BbSfx4Tests`, 9 tests:**
  - forged hits are ≤ 0.25 s, under 5% of their energy above 6 kHz, and have presence above 1 kHz; parry is not a hiss;
  - every forged and feedback cue is linked in the real SfxLibrary;
  - hit budget (3 per window, then room again);
  - a volley makes 2 cues and leaves room for melee;
  - bounces under a hit are dropped, spikes are not;
  - turret ticks are soft and rare;
  - the tap events play their cues;
  - UPGRADE plays level_up or denied;
  - tree ADD / RESET / denied cues.
- All older audio tests (Bb03, BbSfx2, Bb6d, BbView3, BbFix…) keep their expectations.

## Results
- **First full EditMode run with SFX4:** 531 tests, 517 passed, 1 failed, 13 skipped.
  - The failure: `BbView3Tests.SpikeAfterAWallInTheSameTickReplacesTheWallCue`. Its last part plays a plain wall one tick after another wall with a frozen test clock, and the new 60 ms bounce spacing drops it.
  - That part checks "a later tick's spike does not replace an earlier wall", not spacing. The test now steps its clock by `BounceSpacing` first (one line).
  - Its real-match companion (`VerifyOneHazardCuePerBallPerTickInRealMatches`) passed unchanged.
- **Final full EditMode run** (BB-12g + tuning, UI1, SFX4 together): **531 tests, 518 passed, 0 failed, 13 skipped.**
