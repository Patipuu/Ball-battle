# BB-14: two tribute balls — BGLGXLQ (ring of five colours) and HUNTER (snake hunting prey)

Status: **implemented, uncommitted.** Sim change: **Rules 14 → 15**. Design: `claude/bb14-tribute-design.md` (project) — this report lists what was built and where it differs.

## What the player gets
- **BGLGXLQ** (`chorus`, pink):
  - Five colour orbs circle the ball. Each melee hit lights the next colour, in the order PINK → GREEN → BLUE → YELLOW → RED.
  - While lit: PINK heals on each hit, GREEN raises top speed, BLUE gives a one-hit shield, YELLOW adds damage, RED burns.
  - Lighting RED fires **UNITY**: a five-colour ring hits every enemy within 110 px for 4 + 2 × rings done. Then the ring starts again, and each completed ring adds +1.25 melee damage for good.
  - Each colour plays one bell note (C D E G A), so a full ring is a little phrase. UNITY plays a major chord.
  - Skill **HYMN**: lights the next 2 colours.
    - A, CHORUS: UNITY heals 1.5% / 2.25% of max HP.
    - B, RADIANCE: UNITY ×1.6 / ×1.9 and +30 px radius.
  - Tree:
    - DEVOTION: colour effects +6% per rank.
    - MORN LIGHT: start with PINK lit.
    - EVERLIGHT: PINK stays lit after UNITY.
    - 2X RAINBOW: UNITY fires twice at 75%.
- **HUNTER** (`serpent`, teal; the name is a placeholder until Phu gives his friend's name):
  - The arena drops prey: the first at 1.5 s, then one every 3 s, at most 3 on the field. Prey only appear in fights with this ball, so all other fights are unchanged.
  - The ball steers gently toward the nearest prey (1.2° per tick).
  - Eating grows the tail (max 8 segments, +1 HP each meal).
  - The tail whips enemies it touches (0.7 per segment, every 0.5 s).
  - The bite deals 1 + 0.3 per segment + 0.06 per meal ever eaten.
  - An enemy **blade** touching the tail cuts it there. The lost segments drop back as prey, and the tail cannot be cut again for 1 s.
  - Skill **STRIKE** (1.5 s): hunts the enemy instead of prey; the next bite is ×1.5.
    - A, CONSTRICT: with 3+ segments the tail hits ×1.3 and slows the enemy by 25%.
    - B, SWALLOW: eats prey from further away during STRIKE.
  - Tree:
    - SCENT: hunts harder.
    - FIRST MEAL: start with 2 segments.
    - HYDRA: a bite heals 0.2 per segment.
    - SHED SKIN: a cut keeps a quarter of the lost tail.
- **Both balls everywhere:**
  - Run (start choices and enemies), Versus (5 × 2 grid), Codex (10 balls), BALLS page, skill tree with 6 own icons each, chests and levels.
  - **Unlocks:** BGLGXLQ with the first boss (BEAT SHELL KING), HUNTER with the first run win.
- **Art:** placeholder ball sprites (BGLGXLQ: pale disc with the five colours; HUNTER: green snake face), blades, 38/43/48 px sizes, skill and role icons. They will be replaced by Phu's logo and his friend's photo, converted to 32×32 pixel art.

## Balance
- **Versus calibration** (before tuning the Run, 200 seeds × both sides against every ball):
  - BGLGXLQ averages **49.1%** (start damage 1.5, +1.25 per ring, UNITY 4 + 2 per ring). It swings by opponent: strong against blade balls, weak against SPARKLER, HIVE and WRECKER, which starve its ring.
  - HUNTER averages **48.2%**. Prey eaten per fight is about 60, cuts about 24. The changes that got it there: 1 s cut immunity, permanent growth per meal, bite +0.3 per segment, tail 0.7. It is weakest against ANGLER (18%), whose long line keeps cutting the tail.
  - EMBER's average for reference: 49.2%.
- **Run** (greedy bot, seeds 3001–3200, LV1 + 18 LV10 builds), 2 tuning passes:
  - Pass 1:
    - CHORUS heal 6/9% → 3/4.5%; RADIANCE ×1.2/1.35 → ×1.4/1.6 and +30 px; EVERLIGHT keeps PINK only;
    - Run baselines +0.3 damage (BGLGXLQ) and +0.2 bite (HUNTER);
    - CONSTRICT at 3 segments, ×1.3 tail, slow ×0.75; SHED SKIN half → a third.
  - Pass 2: CHORUS heal → 1.5/2.25%; RADIANCE → ×1.6/1.9; 2X RAINBOW 60% → 75%; SHED SKIN → a quarter.
