Each supported ball binds one independent SkillState at spawn. Its level, branch, active state, cooldown, expiry, charges and fire count participate in the deterministic hash. Skills advance once per ActiveTick before movement, so hit-stop pauses both clocks; the first use waits a full cooldown, and condition skills wait indefinitely until eligible. Weapon hooks apply the authored effects, with separate unbuffed Pike growth, charged hit consumption, extra-volley damage, poison-owner healing shares and accelerated turret volleys. SkillFired/SkillEnded carry ball, skill, level, branch and ActiveTick into View/trace. Reflected projectiles retain reflector ownership, cannot reflect again and skip on-hit hooks; Reflect damage uses the existing non-hit pipeline. Normal bosses have no skill; MIMIC copies the player's effective skill specification. Production Sim math remains Fix.
